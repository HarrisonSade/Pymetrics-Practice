Pymetrics

Pymetrics practice

Pymetrics uses short neuroscience-based games to profile cognitive and emotional traits. There are no right answers: each game measures where you sit on a trait scale. Play naturally, stay consistent and read each set of instructions carefully.

Risk

Balloons

Pump balloons for money and bank before they pop. It measures risk tolerance and how you learn from losses.

Fairness

Money Exchange

Give or split money with a partner. It measures trust, altruism and your sense of fairness.

Effort

Keypresses

Press a key as fast as you can for easy or hard tasks. It measures motivation and effort allocation.

Attention

Arrows

Respond to arrow direction or colour as the rules switch. It measures task switching and processing speed.

Emotion

Faces

Identify the emotion on a face. It measures emotional intelligence.

Planning

Towers

Rearrange discs in the fewest moves. It measures planning and problem solving.

Memory

Digits

Recall growing strings of numbers. It measures working memory.

Learning

Cards

Pick from decks with hidden payoffs. It measures learning from feedback and risk preference.

Detail

Lengths

Spot which face has a longer mouth. It measures attention to detail and response to rewards.

Impulsivity

Stop

Press when you see red, but not when you see green. It measures impulse control.
